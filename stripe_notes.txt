Role Upgrade on Payment:
       - $5 payment temporarily upgrades a normal user's role to admin for 5 minutes, granting full admin access.
       - After 5 minutes, the role reverts to the original user role.
Subscription Plan:
       - Recurring payments are supported.
       - On the 4th payment, users receive an advanced plan, extending the role upgrade to 10 minutes at no extra cost.
       - Subsequent payments revert to the standard 5-minute upgrade.
Webhook Integration:
       - Webhooks trigger role updates upon payment.
       - A scheduler ensures the role reverts after the designated time.
